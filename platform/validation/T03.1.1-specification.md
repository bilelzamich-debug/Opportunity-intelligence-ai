## 3. What is built (the three acceptance criteria)

### AC1 — Claims interpretable without reading the Evidence (F-V3)

`oip/extraction.py` provides `extract(request, *, store, log, clock)`:

- The caller (the model-driven extractor per PKP v2 / S-5) submits one
  **ExtractionRequest per claim**. One request carries the complete S-3
  decomposition — `subject`, `predicate`, `qualifier` (explicit `NONE` when
  unqualified), `value` + `value_text` — plus `qualifying_context`,
  `claim_type`, `attributed_to` (when an opinion), the `anchor` (verbatim
  source span), and `extraction_confidence`. **Nothing is defaulted.** A claim
  that cannot state its components cannot be constructed (S-3 alignment; the
  M-19 answer is structural, not editorial).
- The produced `Fact` carries the claim, the qualifying context, its single
  Evidence attachment (with anchor and per-extraction confidence), and an
  N-13 explanation naming the criteria applied. F-V3 is enforced twice: at
  `Fact` construction and again by the store's acceptance path.
- Self-containment is *demonstrated*, not merely claimed: the verifier and
  tests reconstruct what each Fact asserts from the Fact alone.

### AC2 — qualifying_context preserved

- `qualifying_context` is **required, non-empty, and carried verbatim** onto
  the Fact. There is no default and no normalisation.
- Uncertainty is preserved, never silently resolved: a low
  `extraction_confidence` is recorded as supplied (no clamping); an ambiguous
  anchor (span occurs more than once) is **refused**, never guessed; an
  unqualified claim records qualifier `NONE` explicitly (S-3).

### AC3 — Extraction density consistent across comparable evidence

- Density is **measured and published**, never used as a gate (N-22 posture:
  reports are descriptive). The `ExtractionReport` records, per Evidence:
  claims extracted, refusals by stage, content size, and density as claims
  per 1,000 characters (character-based, so the metric is language-agnostic
  for CJK/RTL content; a whitespace word count is reported secondarily).
- The report stratifies by `source_type` (N-20's closed eight-member
  taxonomy). Consistency is *demonstrated on the corpus*: every comparable
  Evidence yields ≥1 claim, and the verifier bounds the spread of densities
  across the eight Evidence objects acquired in the P2 exit corpus.
- What consistency *cannot* mean here is stated honestly: extraction
  judgement is the caller's (model-driven, S-5 "Known Tensions"), so the
  platform's duty is to measure, surface, and publish density — not to pad
  it. A zero-claim Evidence is a flagged anomaly, never silence (N-10).

## 4. The mechanical gates (S-5 Layer 1 at extraction time)

`extract()` refuses, with a recorded N-10 failure, when any of the following
fails. **Fail-closed throughout: no Fact exists unless every gate passed.**

| Gate | Stage (closed set) | Rejects |
|---|---|---|
| Request is an `ExtractionRequest` | `INVALID_REQUEST` | programming errors; malformed requests cannot exist (validated at construction) |
| Evidence resolves and is ACTIVE | `EVIDENCE_NOT_FOUND` / `EVIDENCE_NOT_EXTRACTABLE` | dangling refs, non-Evidence refs, extraction from RETRACTED/INVALIDATED/SUPERSEDED material |
| Storage mode is FULL | `EVIDENCE_NOT_EXTRACTABLE` | REFERENCE-mode material: the platform cannot re-read it in place, so the Layer-1 duty cannot be discharged (N-15 records exactly this exposure) |
| Content non-empty | `EMPTY_CONTENT` | nothing to extract — distinguishable from a failed extraction (N-10) |
| Anchor span occurs **exactly once** | `ANCHOR_NOT_FOUND` / `AMBIGUOUS_ANCHOR` | fabricated locations; ambiguous locations (never guessed) |
| `subject`, `predicate`, and — where present — `value_text` appear in the span (casefold, the `AnchorVerifier._missing_components` semantics) | `UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM` | unsupported inference: claims attributed to spans that do not contain them; fabricated quantities |
| Store acceptance (F-V1..F-V6 + universal rules) | `STORE_REJECTED` | anything the acceptance path refuses; the store keeps its own failure record; no partial trace |

The component check reimplements the S-5 Layer-1 predicate locally (import
budget: ≤6 oip modules, exit-gate constraint); `verify_t03_1_1.py` proves
mechanically, on randomised (claim, span) samples, that the local predicate
agrees with `AnchorVerifier._missing_components` on every sample — the same
"duck-typed at the ratified surface; contract pinned by tests and the
verifier" pattern used by `T02.2.1` for its registers.

**Explicit limit (quoted from S-5):** Layer 1 catches fabricated *location*
and fabricated *values*. It does **not** catch paraphrase drift. Fidelity of
meaning is Layer 2 (sampled audit, `T03.2.2`) and the published rate is
Layer 3 (`T03.2.3`). M-20/M-67 remain open.

## 5. Confidence [R-3, IOM §3.2]

- `assertion_confidence` = the request's `extraction_confidence`
  (extraction certainty for *this* claim, per IOM).
- `evidential_support` = the source Evidence's `effective_confidence`
  (one attachment: support is exactly what its one source carries — IOM:
  "a Fact from a single weak source cannot exceed that source's confidence").
- `effective_confidence` = min of the two, upstream ceiling applied again on
  the acceptance path (V5). No value is invented; nothing is conflated.

## 6. What is deliberately NOT implemented

- **T03.1.2** structured decomposition *as a capability* — the caller states
  the S-3 components; automation is that task's deliverable.
- **T03.1.3** positional anchoring — the anchor at T03.1.1 is the **verbatim
  source span** (the only locator that is mechanically resolvable today).
  Character-offset/line machinery is that task's deliverable.
- **T03.1.4** merging and DUPLICATES recording — extraction never merges
  (under-merging is the S-3-safe direction). Equivalence against existing
  ACTIVE Facts is *assessed and reported* in the outcome
  (`assess_equivalence`, the S-3 four-condition test), and nothing more.
- **T03.1.5** claim-type *classification* — the caller states ASSERTION vs
  ATTRIBUTED_OPINION (F-V4 requires it at construction); automated
  classification is that task's deliverable.
- **T03.2.1–3** Layer-1-at-acceptance wiring for all Facts, sampled audit,
  published rates.
- **No new semantics**: no rights judgement (N-21/N-24 are acquisition-time),
  no independence inference (N-16/T02.1.3 explicit-input model), no density
  gate, no coverage effect (N-22: reports are never gates).
